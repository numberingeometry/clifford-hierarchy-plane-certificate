Let $\mathcal G\cong\F_2^r$ be a group, modulo signs, of commuting traceless Hermitian three-qubit Clifford involutions and $I$, in which every $g\ne I$ is a Pauli or has four-term support spanning a Lagrangian, and which lies in no Pauli MASA\@. Then, after a left Clifford conjugation and a change of generators, $\mathcal G$ is generated by
\[
\hat X_1,\hat X_2,\qquad\text{or}\qquad \hat X_1,\hat X_2,Z_3,\qquad\text{or}\qquad \hat X_1,\hat X_2,\hat X_3,
\]
where $\hat X_j=QX_jQ$ and $Q=\CCZ$.
